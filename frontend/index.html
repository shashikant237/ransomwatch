Academic Disclosure & Scope Limitation

The Isolation Forest model is an unsupervised anomaly detector trained on synthetic behavioral feature vectors modeling both benign baseline tasks and ransomware burst signatures. It identifies multi-dimensional spatial outliers rather than deterministic malware signatures. High performance on synthetic test benchmarks demonstrates algorithmic functionality, not universal immunity against novel evasion techniques or unmodeled legitimate enterprise workflows.
